FAQ

What is transformational life coaching?

Transformational life coaching is a collaborative process designed to reconnect you with the guidance of your inner wisdom, courage, and integrity. From this space, clarity comes naturally and life is navigated with more ease and flow.

How do I know if life coaching is right for me?

Life coaching may be right for you if you feel dissatisfied with any aspect of your life, or simply sense that something is ready to change. This can include difficult life transitions, conflict in relationships, the search for clarity or purpose, thought or behavioral loops, self-actualization, or simply the yearning for more. Trust your curiosity! You do not need to know what you want before you begin. Often, the desire for something to shift is enough.

What makes your approach unique?

Lasting change doesn't come from becoming someone new. It comes from remembering who you've always been beneath fear, conditioning, and self-doubt.

My coaching supports this process by bringing you back to embodiment— tuning into the wisdom of your own body as a source of guidance. My approach combines practical coaching tools with deep reflection, intuition, and dream work (when appropriate). My role is not to provide answers, but to help you unlock your own.

What is a certified life coach?

A certified life coach has completed professional training in coaching skills, ethics, communication, and client support. Certification provides a foundation for effective coaching, while each coach brings their own life experience, perspective, and approach to the work.

What is an ICF-certified life coach?

An ICF-certified life coach has completed professional training through a program approved by the International Coaching Federation, along with other certification requirements including at least 100 hours of coaching experience.

ICF-certification requires ongoing continuing education which offers additional assurance that the coach maintains qualifications and professional standards.

How is coaching different from counseling or therapy?

Coaching and therapy can both be valuable, but serve different purposes. Therapy generally addresses mental health concerns, while coaching focuses primarily on personal growth and forward movement.

Coaching is not a substitute for therapy or mental health treatment, but can complement it beautifully.

Why can't I just use ChatGPT or another AI to coach me?

AI can be a useful tool for reflection, brainstorming, and gathering information but coaching offers something entirely different and irreplaceable: a genuine human relationship, presence, intuition, powerful inquiry, accountability, and the ability to listen deeply to what may not be obvious on the surface.

Human coaching allows you to be seen, heard, reflected, and supported by another person in a holistic way.

What can I expect during a coaching session?

Each session is a thoughtful conversation focused on what is most important to you. Together we explore challenges, patterns, goals, and possibilities through thoughtful inquiry and proven coaching techniques. By the end of each session, we reflect on the insights we've uncovered and identify meaningful next steps. You can expect a supportive, non-judgmental environment where curiosity, reflection, and authentic action are encouraged.

Is coaching confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is an important part of creating a safe and supportive coaching relationship. Information shared during our sessions is treated with care and respect, subject to the terms outlined in the coaching agreement.

What is Dream Work?

Dream Work offers a valuable pathway to explore dreams beyond traditional dream analysis. We engage with the dream on an experiential level—tapping into dream symbols as parts of yourself that offer important perspective and guidance.

This transformative work is done in the safe and confidential container of a coaching session, where insights from your dreams can then be applied to your everyday life and challenges.

How does Dream Work relate to life coaching?

Dream Work offers a valuable pathway to self-discovery and transformation that may be unavailable otherwise.

Dreams often reflect aspects of ourselves that are not fully visible in everyday awareness. They can provide extraordinary insight into patterns, challenges, opportunities, and areas of growth. But insight alone is often not enough. The experiential process of Dream Work combined with life coaching is a potent way to integrate insight through mind, body, and spirit so that change happens holistically, deeply, and sustainably.

Do I need to remember my dreams to benefit from Dream Work?

Not necessarily. A dream fragment or even an image can provide rich material for exploration. We can also discuss ways to better remember and record your dreams so that you will have more to work with.

How many sessions will I need?

This will vary depending on your goals, circumstances, and preferences. Some clients benefit from a single session focused on a specific issue, while most choose an ongoing coaching relationship that allows for deeper exploration and sustained support.
