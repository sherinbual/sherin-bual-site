About

Hello, and welcome.

I’m Sherin, an ICF-credentialed Associate Certified Coach (ACC) and transformational life coach.

I am formally trained in Wayfinder Life Coaching and Advanced Parts Work. My practice is also informed by Internal Family Systems (IFS) and Dream Alchemy, which explores the symbolic language of dreams.

Through these approaches, I help people reconnect with their inner wisdom, deepen self-awareness, and live with greater clarity, integrity, and joy. I also draw from the well of my own experience.

For much of my life, I was searching for something I couldn't quite name.

From the outside, my life looked successful. I earned a degree in psychology, graduated at the top of my law school class, became an attorney, and later completed certifications in yoga, health coaching, and hypnotherapy.

Yet beneath each new chapter was an unshakeable sense that something was missing.

Over time, I came to understand that something wasn’t missing; rather, something essential was waiting to be discovered.

What I was searching for wasn't another achievement—it was myself.

We shall not cease from exploration
And the end of all our exploring
Will be to arrive where we started
And know the place for the first time.

— T. S. Eliot

I call my coaching “transformational” because I help people reconnect with their truest self—the one whose voice is often drowned out by the din of opinions and influences that shape us throughout our lives. We often sacrifice this self at the altar of expectation, obligation, fear, and the many roles and masks we wear.

When we disconnect from who we really are, we pay the price. It can manifest as anything from mild discontent to complete disorientation. It may show up as rage that seems to come out of nowhere, or resentment that quietly seeps into our relationships and choices. We may continue moving through life—doing and accomplishing—while feeling strangely absent from our own experience. We may have everything we thought we wanted and still feel empty.

When we find our way back, no matter how long our absence, we begin to shift. Life shifts. It is one movement.

Sometimes the movement is almost imperceptible, like tectonic plates deep beneath the ocean's surface.

Other times, it is palpable—a strong, relentless current.

And sometimes, insight arrives all at once: a tsunami that clears the path and alters the course of our lives.

Whether subtle and quiet, steady and cumulative, or big and timeline-jumping, we are no longer seeing the world from the same place.

“Because whenever you think or you believe or you know, you’re a lot of other people: but the moment you feel, you’re nobody-but-yourself.”

— E. E. Cummings

As we reconnect with ourselves, our perception of life reorganizes. We make decisions that align with our integrity. We experience more clarity and ease.

We begin living from the inside out.

The greatest gift of this transformation is joy. Not because life becomes perfect, but because we become more fully ourselves, doing what we came here to do. This has a ripple effect, extending beyond our personal and professional lives into our families, communities, society—even the world at large.

“You were made to be yourselves. You were made to enrich the world with a sound, a tone, a shadow.”

— Hermann Hesse

This understanding wasn't born from theory. It emerged through years of self-exploration, life transitions, health challenges, and a visceral need to understand the relationship between mind, body, and spirit.

My younger self could have used transformational life coaching. Rather than navigating life's turbulent waters by my own guiding light, I found myself drifting in the fog of other people's opinions and judgements. In those moments of greatest crisis and confusion, I craved the integrity of my own stewardship.

“Just keep coming home to yourself, you are the one who you've been waiting for.”

— Byron Katie (attributed)

During my journey home, I reimmersed myself in psychology. I plumbed the depths of spirituality, metaphysics, contemplative traditions, and the many ways human beings make meaning of their lives.

Most of all, I studied myself—my thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and how my inner world responds to life. No other inquiry brought greater transformation.

In the fullness of time, all of these paths converged in coaching.
