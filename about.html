About

Hello, and welcome.

For much of my life, I was searching for something I couldn't quite name.

From the outside, my life looked successful. I graduated at the top of my law school class, became an attorney, and later earned certifications in yoga, health coaching, and hypnotherapy.

Yet beneath each new chapter was a quiet sense that something was missing.

Over time, I came to understand that something wasn’t missing; rather, something essential was waiting to be discovered. What I was searching for wasn't another achievement—it was myself.

We shall not cease from exploration
And the end of all our exploring
Will be to arrive where we started
And know the place for the first time.

— T. S. Eliot

I call my coaching “transformational” because I guide people back to their truest self—the one untouched and unburdened by the voices and influences that shape us throughout our lives. We often sacrifice our truest self to expectation, obligation, fear, and the many roles and masks we wear.

When we disconnect from who we really are, the price we pay ranges from mild discontent to complete disorientation. We may continue moving through life, achieving and accomplishing, while feeling strangely absent from our own experience.

When we find our way back, no matter how long our absence, we begin to shift.

Sometimes the shifts are almost imperceptible, unfolding quietly over time. Sometimes they arrive all at once and alter the course of our lives. Whether subtle and quiet, steady and cumulative, or big and timeline-jumping, we are no longer seeing the world from the same place.

As we reconnect with ourselves, our perception of life reorganizes. We make decisions in alignment with our integrity. We experience more clarity and ease. We begin living from the inside out.

The greatest gift of this transformation is greater joy.

Not because life becomes perfect, but because we become more fully ourselves, doing what we came here to do. This tends to have a ripple effect, not only in our personal and professional lives, but within family, community, society, and even the world at large.

This understanding wasn't born from theory. It emerged through years of self-exploration, life transitions, health challenges, and a deep curiosity about the relationship between mind, body, and spirit.

My younger self would have profoundly benefited from transformational life coaching. So many versions of myself would have, because through every crisis or confusion, I craved the integrity of my own stewardship. Instead of navigating the turbulent waters with my own guiding light leading the way, it was dimmed and distorted in the fog of other people’s opinions and judgements.

During my journey home, I re-immersed myself in psychology (my undergraduate degree), along with spirituality, metaphysics, contemplative traditions, and the many ways human beings make meaning of their lives. Most of all, I studied myself—my thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and how my inner world responds to life. No other subject brought greater transformation.

Eventually, all of those paths converged in coaching.

What I once believed was the winter of my life became an unexpected spring. I experienced a sudden clarion call: I had work undone in the world, a world in transition. It was time to leave the safe tower of self-reflection and start a new career that felt a lifetime in the making.

Autumn is a second spring, when every leaf is a flower.

— Albert Camus

Today, I help people reconnect with the quiet wisdom that exists within.

Sometimes that happens through focused conversation. Sometimes it appears through the symbolic language of dreams. However it emerges, my role is not to provide answers, but to help you discover what is already alive inside of you.

I think of coaching as helping people rediscover their internal compass. Even when life feels uncertain or disorienting, that compass remains, waiting to guide you home: that seemingly faraway place on the horizon, where your integrity lives and breathes.

The map, the boat, and all else will fall into place once we wholeheartedly start the journey.
