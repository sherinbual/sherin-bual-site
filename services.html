Services

Every coaching journey is unique.

Sometimes clarity comes through conversation.

Sometimes wisdom is clothed in the language of dreams.

Whether through conversation or dreams, my intention is always the same:
to help you understand yourself more deeply so you can navigate life's challenges with greater clarity, ease, and integrity.

I offer two complementary ways we can work together.
You may choose one or both.

Life Coaching Session

Transformational coaching for times in your life when something is asking to change.

We begin with a challenge or area of life you'd like to shift. Together, we create space to explore what you sense, what you know, and what has yet to come into view. Through powerful questions and coaching tools, we are able to loosen the beliefs and conditioning that tether you to old patterns of limitation and suffering.

What emerges when you are free from these heavy moorings can change the way you see yourself, the world—and what you believe is possible.

60 minutes $150

Dream Work Session

Exploring what your dreams may reveal and how they can illuminate your waking life.

We begin with a dream, a dream fragment, or even a single symbol. Together, we enter the dream and explore its symbols, using questions and imagination to discover what each one may have to reveal. Sometimes we revisit a moment in the dream, change what happens, or allow the dream to continue to a different ending. We then reflect on what we've discovered and how those insights might speak to your waking life.

Often, a single dream continues to open up new possibilities long after the session ends.

75 minutes $175
