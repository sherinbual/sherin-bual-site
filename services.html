Services

Every coaching journey is unique.

Sometimes clarity comes through conversation.

Sometimes wisdom is clothed in the language of dreams.

Whether through conversation or dreams, my intention is the same: to help you understand yourself more deeply so you can navigate life's challenges with greater clarity, ease, and integrity.

I offer two ways we can work together.

Life Coaching Session

Transformational coaching for navigating change, relationships, purpose, decisions, and personal growth.

We begin with a challenge or area of life you'd like to shift. Together, we explore the issue through powerful questions and coaching tools that cultivate awareness, insight, and intuition. We conclude by reflecting on what has emerged and how you can carry it forward into everyday life.

Whether you come for one session or many, the work is designed to meet you where you are and support whatever comes next.

Dream Work Session

A life coaching session that uses dreams as a doorway into deeper self-understanding and transformation.

We begin with a dream, a fragment of a dream, or even a single symbol. Using imaginative techniques, we access the wisdom and intuition held within your deepest self. We then explore how to apply these messages to whatever challenge or situation you're facing.

This is a surprisingly rich and potent process that can be a profound one-time experience or become a meaningful thread woven throughout an ongoing coaching journey.
